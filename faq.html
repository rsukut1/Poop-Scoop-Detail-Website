Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dog waste removal services in the Boise area.

What does your service include?
Our standard service includes full pet waste removal from your yard and responsible disposal. We handle everything from start to finish so you don’t have to worry about cleanup or disposal.
Do you charge extra for waste disposal?
No. Waste removal and disposal are included in all standard services. We do not leave waste behind or charge separate disposal fees.
What areas do you serve?
We currently serve Boise, Idaho.
How often can I schedule service?
We offer one-time cleanups, weekly service, and bi-weekly service depending on your needs.
Are your products safe for pets and children?
Yes. We use carefully selected, manufacturer-labeled products that are safe for pets and children. We prioritize eco-friendly and non-toxic solutions whenever possible.
Are there any additional fees or special conditions?
Our standard service includes full cleanup and disposal. In certain conditions—such as heavily saturated yards, unusually high contamination, or hazardous materials like needles—additional time or safety precautions may be required. We always communicate clearly before proceeding.
Do I need to be home during service?
No. Most customers are not home during service. As long as we have yard access, we can complete the work and notify you when it’s finished.
How quickly will I get a response to a quote request?
We respond to all quote requests within 24 hours.
